# Nutrition & Recovery Reference

> Note: This is secondary guidance. Core focus remains on training periodization and analysis.

## Nutrition Periodization

### By Training Phase

| Phase | Carb Intake | Protein | Fat | Key Focus |
|-------|-------------|---------|-----|-----------|
| Base | Moderate | 1.4-1.6g/kg | 25-30% | Support recovery |
| Development | Higher | 1.6-1.8g/kg | 20-25% | Fuel quality sessions |
| Specificity | High | 1.6-2.0g/kg | 20% | Practice race nutrition |
| Taper | Moderate→High | 1.4-1.6g/kg | 20-25% | Glycogen loading |

### Session-Specific Fueling

**Before Training**:
- Long/intense: 1-4h before, carb-rich meal
- Easy: can train fasted for metabolic adaptation
- Key session: prioritize fueling

**During Training**:
- <90min easy: water only acceptable
- >90min: 30-60g carbs/hour
- Long runs: practice race nutrition

**After Training**:
- 30min window: protein + carbs
- Target: 20-30g protein, 0.8-1g/kg carbs
- Rehydration: 150% of fluid lost

## Race Day Nutrition

### Pre-Race (24-48h)
- Carb loading: 8-10g/kg/day
- Reduce fiber
- Familiar foods only
- Hydrate well (urine pale)

### Race Fueling Rates

| Duration | Cal/hour | Carbs/hour | Notes |
|----------|----------|------------|-------|
| <3h | 30-60g | 30-60g | Gels/drinks sufficient |
| 3-6h | 200-300 | 50-80g | Mix gels + solid |
| 6-12h | 200-300 | 50-70g | Increase solid food |
| >12h | 150-250 | 40-60g | Real food essential |

### Hydration
- 400-800ml/hour depending on conditions
- Sodium: 300-700mg/hour
- Don't overdrink (hyponatremia risk)

## Recovery Protocols

### Post-Training

**Immediate (0-30min)**:
- Protein shake or recovery drink
- Begin rehydration
- Light stretching if desired

**Short-term (2-4h)**:
- Complete meal with protein, carbs, vegetables
- Continue hydration
- Compression if available

**Evening**:
- Prioritize sleep
- Avoid alcohol (impairs recovery)
- Light mobility if stiff

### Post-Race Recovery

| Race Type | Full Recovery | Return to Training |
|-----------|---------------|-------------------|
| VK | 3-5 days | 1 week |
| Trail Court | 1-2 weeks | 2 weeks |
| Trail Long | 2-3 weeks | 3-4 weeks |
| Ultra | 3-4 weeks | 4-6 weeks |

**Recovery Week Activities**:
- Days 1-3: Walking, swimming, very light activity
- Days 4-7: Easy jogging if feeling good
- Week 2+: Gradual return based on feel

## Mental Preparation

### Race Week
- Visualize course, key sections
- Plan aid station strategy
- Review contingency plans
- Reduce life stress where possible

### During Race
- Break into segments (aid to aid)
- Mantras for difficult moments
- Focus on process, not outcome
- Accept discomfort as part of the challenge

### Low Points
- Normal and expected
- Usually pass within 20-30min
- Eat, drink, adjust pace
- Talk to others if helpful
- Remember: "It never always gets worse"

---

## Sleep Optimization

### Training Period
- 7-9 hours minimum
- Consistent schedule
- Cool, dark room
- Limit screens before bed

### Race Week
- Extra sleep if possible (bank)
- Don't stress about night-before sleep
- Nap if needed (before 3pm)

### During Ultra
- Micro-naps: 10-20min can help
- Caffeine: save for night sections
- Eyes: sunglasses reduce fatigue
- Plan for circadian low points (2-5am)

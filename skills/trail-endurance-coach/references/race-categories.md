# Race Categories Reference

## Category Definitions

| Category | Distance | Typical Duration | Key Demands |
|----------|----------|------------------|-------------|
| VK (Vertical Kilometer) | 1-5km, 1000m D+ | 30-60 min | Pure climbing power, VO2max |
| Trail Court | <40km | 2-5 hours | Threshold capacity, technique |
| Trail Long | 40-80km | 5-12 hours | Endurance, nutrition, pacing |
| Ultra | >80km | 12-30+ hours | Ultra endurance, mental, nutrition |

---

## Vertical Kilometer (VK)

### Race Profile
- 1000m elevation gain
- Distance: 2-5km typically
- Gradient: 20-50% average
- Duration: 30-60 min for elites, 45-90 min amateur

### Physiological Demands
- **Primary**: VO2max, lactate threshold
- **Secondary**: Muscular power, mental tolerance
- **Limiting factors**: Quad fatigue, breathing capacity

### Key Training Sessions
1. **Hill repeats**: 3-5min @ Z4-Z5, steep gradient
2. **Threshold climbs**: 15-25min sustained @ Z4
3. **Power hiking drills**: Efficient pole technique
4. **Short races**: Use shorter VKs as training

### Periodization Notes
- Shorter preparation (8-12 weeks sufficient)
- High intensity focus
- Taper: 10-14 days

---

## Trail Court (<40km)

### Race Profile
- Distance: 20-40km
- D+: 1000-2500m typically
- Duration: 2-5 hours
- Terrain: Variable, technical sections

### Physiological Demands
- **Primary**: Lactate threshold, aerobic capacity
- **Secondary**: Descente technique, muscular endurance
- **Limiting factors**: Pacing errors, technical fatigue

### Key Training Sessions
1. **Long runs**: 2-3h with terrain variety
2. **Tempo efforts**: 30-50min @ Z3-Z4
3. **Fartlek**: Variable intensity on trails
4. **Descente technique**: Progressive exposure
5. **Back-to-back days**: Weekend cumulative fatigue

### Race Execution
- Start conservative (Z2-Z3)
- Push threshold on climbs mid-race
- Manage descentes (quad preservation)
- Nutrition: 200-300 cal/hour, practice in training

### Periodization Notes
- 10-16 weeks ideal preparation
- Balance volume and intensity
- Taper: 2 weeks

---

## Trail Long (40-80km)

### Race Profile
- Distance: 40-80km
- D+: 2000-5000m
- Duration: 5-12 hours
- Cutoffs: Often present, pace management critical

### Physiological Demands
- **Primary**: Aerobic endurance, fat oxidation
- **Secondary**: Muscular durability, nutrition execution
- **Limiting factors**: GI issues, accumulated muscle damage, mental

### Key Training Sessions
1. **Long runs**: 3-5h, progressive (build to 30-35km)
2. **Back-to-back weekends**: Sat long + Sun moderate
3. **Night training**: If race includes darkness
4. **Race simulations**: Test nutrition, gear, pacing
5. **Specificity terrain**: Match race profile

### Race Execution
- Extremely conservative start (Z1-Z2 first hours)
- Walk steep climbs early (save quads)
- Systematic nutrition (set timer)
- Aid station efficiency
- Mental segmentation (checkpoint to checkpoint)

### Nutrition Strategy
- 250-350 cal/hour
- Mix solid/liquid
- Sodium: 300-500mg/hour
- Practice everything in training

### Periodization Notes
- 14-20 weeks preparation
- Volume emphasis, then specificity
- Taper: 2-3 weeks

---

## Ultra (>80km)

### Race Profile
- Distance: 80-170km+
- D+: 4000-10000m+
- Duration: 12-36+ hours
- Multiple day/night cycles possible

### Physiological Demands
- **Primary**: Extreme endurance, metabolic flexibility
- **Secondary**: Sleep management, mental resilience
- **Limiting factors**: GI issues, sleep deprivation, cumulative damage

### Key Training Sessions
1. **Very long runs**: 5-8h, terrain specific
2. **Multi-day blocks**: 3 consecutive days of running
3. **Night training**: Essential if race crosses night
4. **Power hiking endurance**: Hours of sustained hiking
5. **Heat/cold exposure**: Environmental preparation
6. **Equipment testing**: Everything you'll use in race

### Race Execution
- "Start slow, then slow down"
- Walk all significant climbs from start
- Eat before hungry, drink before thirsty
- Plan sleep strategy (micro-naps vs push through)
- Crew/pacer coordination if allowed

### Nutrition Strategy
- 200-300 cal/hour (lower intensity = lower burn)
- Variety essential (sweet/savory rotation)
- Real food tolerance varies with fatigue
- Caffeine strategy (save for night/low points)
- Sodium: 400-700mg/hour depending on conditions

### Mental Strategies
- Break into small goals
- Mantras for low points
- Acceptance of suffering
- Focus on controllables
- Visualization of finish

### Periodization Notes
- 20+ weeks preparation
- Very high volume phase required
- Multiple long weekends
- Taper: 3 weeks (significant volume reduction)

## Cross-Category Principles

### Terrain Matching
Always prioritize training on terrain similar to race:
- Rocky technical → practice on rocks
- Steep climbs → find steep local hills
- Exposed ridges → train in wind
- Night sections → night runs

### Elevation Ratio
Calculate race D+/km ratio and match in training:
```
ratio = total_elevation_m / distance_km
```
- Flat trail: <30m/km
- Rolling: 30-50m/km
- Mountainous: 50-80m/km
- Very steep: >80m/km

### Equipment Testing
Test in training, never race day:
- Shoes (multiple options)
- Hydration system
- Poles (if allowed/useful)
- Layers for conditions
- Headlamp (batteries, brightness)
- Nutrition (specific products)
